## ✨ Features

- 🫣 **Step 1: The Ask Out** with the runaway **"no"** button that dodges hover / touch.
- 💅 **Glowing "YES obviously 💅"** button with neon pink pulses & Web Audio sound effects.
- 🎉 **Step 2: Activity Selection** (Fancy dinner, Movie night, Picnic outside, Fun & games, Coffee & vibes, Something artsy).
- 📅 **Step 3: Scheduling / Timing** (This weekend, Next week, Pretty soon, Anytime for you).
- 🥳 **Step 4: Celebration Itinerary & Full-screen Confetti Shower**.
- 📬 **Instant Silent Email Alert**: Choices are automatically submitted to the sender's email via FormSubmit!
- 🪄 **Share with Friends**: Built-in "Create your own link" generator so anyone can generate their own customized date proposal link with their email.
